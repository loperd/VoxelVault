# 0.4.1

The Traveler's Chest has a new setting: a birch grove at golden hour with a three-step stone pedestal, torches and pillars; the chest hangs over the pedestal and lands on it. A creeper, a rabbit, a flock of birds and a falling star still pass through on the flat meadow around it (the creeper stops beside the pedestal).

The panels of the case screen slide in from the top and the bottom, and only then does the chest lower from above and hang. The roulette starts at full motion with no wind-up and spins about half a second longer.

Mobs of the Overworld (creeper, rabbit, villager, sheep) are drawn with a pixel texture instead of flat colour.

A 401 from the inventory or wallet after sign-in now returns the player to the sign-in screen and clears the chests shown, instead of an error page with a stale list.

The End now has more going on: Endermen teleport onto the chest's island one at a time, stare at the chest, shudder and vanish in a puff; End crystals on the spires explode now and then (fireball, debris, a flash and a tremor that reaches the chest); and the dragon flies through the scene, sweeping low over the chest every 15–25 seconds. The dragon model is shared with the Dragon's Reach scene.

The Traveler's Chest can drop a new legendary item, the Knight privilege, shown as a voxel knight in 3D. Its sources and build script now live in the repository (assets/items/knight, scripts/items/knight), and meshoptimizer is a direct development dependency.
